We selected one two-unit monoGRU model with ReLU activations and no decoder bias per subject and outer fold by minimizing block-reset training-plus-validation negative log-likelihood (11 subjects, ten outer folds). Models were initially balanced by minimizing their total squared parameter norm under function-preserving positive hidden-unit rescaling, and units were ordered by decoder contrast so that $h_1$ favored choice~1 and $h_2$ favored choice~0. The population medoid minimized the equally subject-weighted mean Euclidean distance over candidate-input, recurrent, candidate-bias, and decoder parameters in this balanced space, selecting WS14, outer fold~3. Each fold was then aligned to this fixed reference by numerically minimizing the same parameter distance over positive hidden-unit scales, preserving the unit-label convention. The resulting aligned parameters were averaged over outer folds within subjects and then equally over subjects. Equations report these descriptive means, rounded to two significant figures.
